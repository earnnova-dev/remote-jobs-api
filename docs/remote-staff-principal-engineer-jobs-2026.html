Why a feed beats a job board for senior-IC roles

If you're a staff or principal engineer — or building a tool that tracks the senior-IC market — a static job-board page goes stale the moment a listing closes. A feed doesn't. You can:

  • Pull GET /v1/jobs on a schedule and filter client-side to senior-IC titles, surfacing only new roles (dedupe on the stable id).
  • Filter by min_salary to skip roles that won't meet your target comp, or by source to bias toward a board you trust.
  • Rank by fit_score to surface the best-matching roles for a specific senior-IC job description.
  • Get salary where the board publishes it — currently 89 of 327 live roles carry a salary figure (27%), with a median of $104,800 and a top of $500,000.

Or pull the staff/principal slice into a script with the Python standard library — no dependencies:

import json, urllib.request, re

url = "https://remote-jobs-api.tten.no/v1/jobs?limit=500"
with urllib.request.urlopen(url) as r:
    data = json.load(r)

pat = re.compile(r"\b(staff|principal)\b", re.I)
eng = re.compile(r"engineer|software|developer|architect|sre|infrastructure|platform|ml\b", re.I)
for job in data["jobs"]:
    t = job.get("title", "")
    if pat.search(t) and eng.search(t):
        sal = job.get("salary") or "n/a"
        print(f"- {t} | {job['company']} | {job.get('location')} | {sal}")

Useful parameters: skills (comma-separated), source (remotive, remoteok, jobicy, wwr, hn), limit, min_salary, and min_score (minimum fit_score).
